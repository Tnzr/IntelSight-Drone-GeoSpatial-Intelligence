# Business Economics

This directory contains the business, investment, and financial materials for IntelSight.

## Contents

- `business-economic-plan.md` — market demand, cost structure, revenue streams, and investment logic
- `drone-unit-investment-memo.md` — unit economics and buy-vs-build recommendation
- `financial-projections.md` — conservative turnover, cost, margin, and break-even forecast
- `market-positioning.md` — target sectors, differentiation, and commercial strategy

## Objective

The purpose of this directory is to keep the economic case grounded in real operational costs, subscription revenue, and strategic market value rather than hype-driven projections.
